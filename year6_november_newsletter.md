Dear Parents and Carers,

Can you believe we're already in November? The children have been working incredibly hard this term, and we're so proud of the progress they've made. Here's a round-up of everything that's been happening in Maple Class.

---

## Learning Highlights

### English
This month we've been reading *Holes* by Louis Sachar as our class novel. The children have been writing diary entries from Stanley's perspective and exploring themes of justice and friendship. Their work has been outstanding — some of the diary entries were genuinely moving to read!

### Maths
We've moved into fractions, decimals and percentages. Most children are feeling more confident converting between the three, and we've been using real-world examples (recipes, sale prices, sports statistics) to make it feel relevant. Times tables remain important — please do keep encouraging your child to practise at home.

### Science
Our forces and motion unit is in full swing. The children built and tested their own parachute designs last week to investigate air resistance, and the results were brilliant (and occasionally chaotic!). Ask your child which design worked best!

### Topic (History)
We've been exploring World War II, with a focus on the experiences of children during the Blitz. The children have been writing evacuation letters and creating Anderson shelter models. We'll be visiting the **Imperial War Museum on 22nd November** — permission slips went home last week, please return by **Friday 8th November**.
